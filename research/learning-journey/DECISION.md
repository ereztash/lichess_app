# DECISION

## The decision

Build **candidate 4, the pattern-centered learning loop**, with candidate 5 as its empty-state
contract and candidate 3p (procedural prescription) as its voice. Build it as **information
architecture over objects that already exist**, not as a new subsystem.

Rejected, with reasons in `CANDIDATE_ARCHITECTURES.md`: goal-first as an architecture (admitted as
copy only), and managed-effort in its efficacy register.

## Where the plan was wrong

`DR_PLAN_0.md` predicted H-A (information architecture) would survive and E6 would be largely Δ0.
H-A survives **in its conclusion and not in its mechanism**: the plan proposed appending GOAL,
TRANSFER and OUTCOME to the scalar `loopPosition`, and two of those three appends were refuted —
GOAL because framing is not a state, OUTCOME because a named empty state is a proxy magnet. E6 was
not Δ0; it supplied the distinction the whole design now turns on.

## What is built

The smallest slice that makes the loop perceptible end to end:

1. `shared/learning-journey.ts` — the state machine as a pure function over objects already in the
   record. Each stage carries its construct, its denominator and its next act.
2. A ledger surface that renders the plural objects and their stages, registered under LAW 1.
3. `loopPosition` extended so the scalar pointer no longer stops at `grade`, and its
   instrument-vs-person overclaim repaired.
4. The goal stored and rendered as framing copy, with a gate refusing goal-adjacent counts.
5. Tests and positive controls for every new invariant.

## What is deliberately not built

- no global progress number of any kind;
- no rating denominator, and `ratingSeries` stays unrendered in this slice;
- no prescribed training programme;
- no user-selectable coaching style (E5: no evidence it is needed; geolocating a register would be a
  stereotype dressed as personalisation);
- no port of the research discovery vocabulary into the product (that is a research decision with an
  open confound, Q2);
- no onboarding: no usability evidence says the interface cannot teach itself.

## Claim boundary

Everything here is **repository-verified**. That the journey is *understandable* is unproven, and it
is the next thing to test.
