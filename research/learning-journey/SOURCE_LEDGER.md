## What this ledger does not do

It does not establish that any of these sources is right. It establishes what was read, when, and
which decision moved because of it, so that a later reader can go and check — and so that a source
that disappears or changes is a detectable event rather than a silent one.

**Reconstructed after the fact.** The searches ran on 2026-09-12 and this ledger was written the
same day during an assurance pass, from the results of those searches. It is not a capture log. A
future research pass should record the source at the moment the claim is formed, which costs nothing
at the time and cannot be recovered afterwards.

**S1 is the weakest load-bearing row.** It carries a ΔDecision and its primary source could not be
fetched. The direction it establishes — practice success is not game behaviour — is corroborated by
S5 from an independent literature, which is why the decision stands on the pair rather than on S1
alone.
