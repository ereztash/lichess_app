6. THE ALTERNATIVE THIS PASS CANNOT RULE OUT, BOUNDED

   If stronger players' games contain structurally different T- positions, their
   move-blind criterion differs too, and part of the observed shift is composition
   rather than anything psychological. The item-level records are not on disk, so
   this cannot be tested -- only bounded, by asking how much the chance rates would
   have to move to produce the whole shift on their own.

   observed criterion shift, 1200-1400 -> 1800-3500: -0.3704

   if ONLY the T- chance rate drifts:  0.1010 -> 0.2963  (2.93x)
   if BOTH drift equally:              T+ 0.3173 -> 0.4582  (1.44x),  T- 0.1010 -> 0.1826 (1.81x)

   Those are large. Explaining the WHOLE shift by composition is implausible;
   explaining PART of it needs only a modest drift, and part is all it takes to
   make the remainder uninterpretable. NOT RULED OUT.

VERDICT

   RC-06's move-blind floor is d' = 0.8005, against a measured 1.1803 in the lowest band.
   Sensitivity above that floor still orders the rating bands and still has room to
   grow. The CRITERION does not survive as a player parameter: RC-06's two cells
   score different acts, and across the twelve rule classes half the variance in c
   is predicted by predicate geometry alone.

   So the criterion channel is not a thing to intervene on here. It is a thing to
   MEASURE PROPERLY FIRST, on a rule class whose predicate does not branch -- of
   which this screen already contains eleven.
