# Session script

Everything in a blockquote is said **verbatim**. Everything else is instruction to the moderator.

The single most valuable thing this script produces is **the exact state where hesitation,
abandonment or assistance first occurred**. It cannot be reconstructed afterwards. Write it down as
it happens, with the time.

## Before you start

Screening form filled. Recording running if permitted. Participant file open with the clock
started. Their phone in their hand, not yours.

## 0. Opening

Hand over the URL and say this and nothing else.

> תפתח את הקישור ותעשה מה שהיית עושה.
>
> אני לא אסביר כלום, וזה בכוונה. אם משהו לא ברור, זה מה שאני צריך לראות.
>
> תדבר בקול רם כשנוח לך. אם אתה שותק זה בסדר גמור.

Then stop talking. Start the log.

## The assistance ladder

Escalate one step at a time. **Wait 45 seconds of visible stuckness before `A1`, and another 45
before `A2`.** Stuckness means not acting: scrolling back and forth, re-reading, looking up at you.
Reading carefully is not stuckness.

| Level | What it sounds like | What it costs |
|---|---|---|
| `A0` | Nothing said | Measure can pass cold |
| `A1` | *תגלול למטה*. *זה בהמשך הדף*. *יש שם כפתור* | Measure can still pass cold |
| `A2` | Anything that says what the product, a screen or a stage is **for** | **Measure did not pass cold** |
| `A3` | Anything that says what a number, a finding or a result **means** | **Measure did not pass cold** |

Tag against the measure the help touched, not the session. Write the level and the state on the
same log line.

Two phrases to keep ready, because the pull to explain is strong and it is the whole experiment:

> אני מעדיף לא לענות על זה עכשיו. נדבר על זה בסוף.

> מה שנראה לך נכון זה מה שאני רוצה לראות.

## 1. First use, uninterrupted

Say nothing while they read the front door and choose a door. Log every press, every scroll that
goes back up, every pause over three seconds.

If they ask what a control does, that is data. Log the question verbatim and deflect with the
phrases above.

**Log:** which of the three doors they took, and how long the front door held them.

## 2. M1, value comprehension

Ask **after first use** and not before. First use means they have taken a decision and seen what
came back, or they have stopped trying.

> במילים שלך, מה הכלי הזה מנסה לעשות?

Wait. Do not fill the silence.

> ובמה זה שונה ממה שמנוע שחמט רגיל נותן לך?

**Record verbatim, both answers.**

Passes when they name, in their own words, that the decision is recorded before the engine speaks,
or the separation between what the move cost and what happened on the way to choosing it. "מנוע
שמראה לך את הטעויות" is a fail, however confidently it is said.

## 3. M3 and M4, observed throughout

Not questions. At every state change, two facts go in the log:

* did they choose an action without help, and at what assistance level
* did the action they chose complete

If they choose an action the product does not offer, log what they tried to do. That is a next
action the product failed to expose, and it is worth more than a completed one.

## 4. M2, journey orientation

Ask at a mid-session pause, after at least one decision is on the record.

> אם היית צריך להסביר למישהו איפה אתה עכשיו בתוך הכלי, מה היית אומר?

> מה הוא יודע עליך עכשיו?

> ומה הוא עדיין לא יודע?

**Record verbatim.** Passes when they can say what it currently holds and what it does not, without
inventing a claim it has not made.

### The one probe that must not point

Immediately after, still inside M2:

> כמה עוד צריך לעשות עד שיהיה לו מה להגיד?

Record three things:

1. the **number** they say,
2. the **unit** they use, החלטות or משחקים,
3. **which screen they were looking at** when they said it.

Do not mention that there are two counters. Do not ask which one they meant. Do not ask whether it
is confusing. A participant who agrees that two denominators are confusing after being shown that
there are two has told you nothing, and it would spend the only cold reading this build gets.

If they say a number whose unit does not match the denominator on their screen, or if they pause
and visibly hunt between two numbers, log it exactly as it happened.

## 5. M5, evidence boundary

Ask after at least one counter has moved. Open question first.

> אם המספר הזה יעלה, מה זה אומר?

Record it. Then, only if that answer does not already separate the two, ask the frozen question as
written in the protocol:

> זה אומר שהוא יודע עליך יותר, או שאתה שיחקת יותר טוב?

**Record both verbatim.** Any answer treating the counter as a measure of skill is a fail, and the
open answer is what says whether the product caused the confusion or the binary did.

## 6. M6, payoff

Behaviour first. Before asking anything, note whether they took the next unit of effort on their
own. Then:

> שווה לך להמשיך עוד סיבוב?

> מה אתה מצפה לקבל אם תמשיך?

**Record verbatim.** Passes when what they expect is something this product can actually produce.
An expectation the product cannot meet is a pass on enthusiasm and a fail on M6, and the second
reading is the one that counts.

## 7. M7, organic continuation

This is the measure most easily destroyed by the moderator, so it has its own rules.

Take the **first** of these that occurs and note which one it was:

* the first reveal, read to the end;
* the first blitz game that finishes.

Then **say nothing for 60 seconds.** Look at your notes. Do not nod, do not lean in, do not raise
your eyebrows, do not say *אז?*, and do not let the silence become a question. If they ask whether
they should keep going, the answer is:

> מה שאתה רוצה.

Log what happened in the 60 seconds, second by second if you can.

Passes only when they continue without prompting. Continuing because the silence got uncomfortable
is not continuing; if you think that is what happened, write that down instead of a pass.

## 8. M8, negative state

Every participant must meet at least one. The reliable one is a blitz game that finishes, which
returns *"לא מצאתי במשחק הזה לבדו משהו שכדאי להסיק ממנו עליך"*. The record page's shortfall
sentence also qualifies.

When they reach it, before any explanation:

> מה זה אומר?

> ומה עכשיו?

**Record verbatim.** Passes when they can say what the negative result means and what follows from
it. "אז זה לא עבד" or "אין פה כלום" is a fail, and it is a claim-boundary failure rather than a
wording one.

## 9. Close

Only now may you explain anything, and only after the recording of answers is complete.

> יש משהו שרצית לעשות ולא מצאת איך?

> אם היית פותח את זה מחר לבד, מה היית עושה ראשון?

Record both. Then answer whatever they ask, at any level you like. The measurement is over.

## Immediately after they leave

Before you do anything else, fill in the one field that decays fastest:

**The exact state where hesitation, abandonment or assistance first occurred.** Name the screen,
the control they were looking at, and the time.
