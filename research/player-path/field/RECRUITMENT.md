# Recruitment and screening

Target: **3 to 5** cold chess players. Five is better than three; four is enough.

## Who qualifies

Two axes only. Do not screen for enthusiasm, for interest in the idea, or for willingness to give
feedback. A participant recruited because they like the concept is a participant who will work to
make the session succeed.

* **They play chess.** Any level. Online, over the board, club, casual. The spread is a feature.
* **They are outside this project.**

## Who is excluded

* Anyone previously coached through this build.
* Anyone who has seen a walkthrough, a demo or a screenshot of it.
* Anyone who has been in a conversation about its design.
* Anyone who would find it hard to say "I have no idea what this is" to the person running the
  session. This is a real exclusion, not a formality, and it is the reason a close friend or a
  direct report is a poor participant here.

## What they may be told beforehand

One message, and nothing beyond it. Anything more is `A2` before the session has started.

> יש לי כלי שחמט שאני רוצה שמישהו ינסה, בלי שאסביר עליו קודם.
>
> זה לוקח בערך 25 דקות. אתה פותח קישור בטלפון ומשחק, ואני יושב בצד ורושם.
>
> אני לא בודק אותך. אני בודק אם הכלי מסביר את עצמו. כל מקום שבו לא ברור מה לעשות הוא בדיוק מה
> שאני צריך לראות, אז אין טעות שאפשר לעשות כאן.

If they ask what it does, the answer is:

> זה בדיוק מה שאני רוצה לגלות. אני מעדיף לא לענות לפני שתסתכל.

## Screening form

Filled before the session, in the participant's own words where the field says so. Copy into the
participant file.

| Field | Ask like this | Record |
|---|---|---|
| Rating | יש לך דירוג? איפה? | Their number and its source, or "no idea" |
| Existing analysis workflow | מה אתה עושה אחרי משחק, אם בכלל? | **Verbatim.** Do not summarise into a category |
| Improvement intent | יש משהו בשחמט שאתה מנסה להשתפר בו עכשיו? | **Verbatim** |
| Prior knowledge | שמעת פעם על Decision Lab? | none / heard of it / has seen a screen / has used it |
| Device | Their own phone where possible | Model and viewport if obtainable |
| Recording | May I record the screen? | yes / no |

A participant who answers `has used it` or `has seen a screen` is excluded. Record the exclusion
and why; a person who turns out not to be cold is data about how the recruitment channel leaks, not
a wasted slot to be quietly reused.

## Setting

Their own phone, their own hand on the screen. The moderator does not hold the device, does not
scroll it, and does not point at it. If a laptop is unavoidable, note it; the build was audited at
390x844 and a desktop session is a different stimulus.

Sessions are individual. Two participants in one room will orient each other, and the second one is
no longer cold.

## Order

Run one session, write its file, and only then recruit or schedule the next. Do not batch. The
protocol forbids repairing the product between participants, and it does not forbid noticing after
participant 2 that the recruitment message is leaking an explanation.
