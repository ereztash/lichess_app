# Founder as translation layer

The candidate mechanism: the founder currently performs Evidence → Meaning → Action by hand, and
the product's apparent coherence depends on that translation being present.

Not assumed. Audited by asking, of each surface, which of the three the product supplies by itself.

| Surface | Says what was measured | Says what is unknown | Says what evidence is needed | Says **why it matters to the player's goal** | Says **what changed for them** | Says **why the next cost is rational** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Import finding | yes, with both n's | yes | yes | **yes**, and this is the best instance in the product: it names what the retrospective corpus cannot hold and why no quantity fixes it | yes | **yes** |

## The pattern

The product is **consistently strong on the epistemic triple** and **inconsistently weak on the
motivational triple**. Every surface says what was measured and what is not known. Only four of
nine say why the next cost is rational, and only two say why any of it matters to the player's own
goal.

The two surfaces that do it well, the import finding and the resume screen's `because` lines, show
that the product is capable of it. This is therefore not a missing capability. It is an
inconsistently applied one, which is the signature of a translation that usually happens in
conversation.

## Assistance-level mapping

Against the frozen FIELD ladder:

| If the participant needs | Level | What it implies |
|---|---|---|
| "scroll down", "it is further on" | `A1` | navigation only, still a cold pass |
| "this measures what you thought before the engine spoke" | **`A2`** | the value contract did not carry itself. Founder translation at the promise layer |
| "the number means the app has more evidence, not that you improved" | **`A3`** | the claim boundary did not carry itself |
| "keep going because the next one buys X" | **`A3`** | the rationality of the next cost did not carry itself. **This is the level the table above predicts will be needed most** |

## What FIELD would confirm or falsify this

**Confirms it:** participants who pass M1 to M5 at `A0`, and who require `A2` or `A3` specifically
at the moment they are asked to continue, that is, `A3` clustering on M6 and M7 rather than on M1.

**Falsifies it:** participants who need `A2` at the front door, before any evidence has been
produced. That would be a value-contract failure rather than a translation failure, because there
is nothing yet to translate.

The two are distinguishable in the frozen protocol by **where the first assistance was needed**,
which the participant file records as a required field.
