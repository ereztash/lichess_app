# Voice of customer

## Method, and what this file may not be used for

**Search-based, not systematic.** Public forum and review content was searched for the clusters the
mission names. This is **tier 6** evidence — repeated user signal — and it is used for exactly one
purpose: **identifying friction and generating hypotheses.** It is never used as evidence that
anything works.

**Depth is uneven and is marked.** Clusters below carry a `DEPTH` label:
`SEARCHED` = found directly in this pass, with the source class named.
`NOT SEARCHED` = the mission listed it and this pass did not reach it. It is listed as absent
rather than filled in, because a VOC file padded with plausible complaints would be worse than a
short one.

**No counting is offered.** A search engine's result set is not a sample, so "number of independent
examples" would be a fabricated denominator. Where a theme recurred across distinct threads and
products it is called **repeated**; where it appeared once it is called **single**.

## Cluster 1 — "puzzles do not transfer to games" · `SEARCHED` · **repeated**

**Products:** Chess.com Tactics/Puzzles, Lichess Puzzles, general.
**Representative statements** (paraphrased from public forum threads):

> *"In games there is nothing telling you that there is a tactic there, whereas in a puzzle you know
> there is something there."*

> *"Puzzles train solving, while real games require detecting that a tactic exists."*

> *"2400 puzzle rating but stuck in the mid-1200s rapid."*

**Why this cluster is the most important thing in this file.** Players have independently
articulated the **focal-cue distinction** (V8) without the vocabulary for it. The stated mechanism —
*a puzzle announces itself, a game does not* — is exactly the boundary between cued application
(L4) and uncued transfer (L5), and it converges with the prospective-memory literature from an
entirely independent direction.

**Counterexamples found:** yes, and they matter. Several responses attribute the gap to *scope*
rather than cueing — puzzles train tactics only, while games need openings, endgames, strategy and
clock management. That is a different explanation for the same complaint, and this pass cannot
separate them.

**Status: hypothesis-generating, and it happens to agree with a tier-1 source.** It is not evidence
that a focal-trigger intervention would work.

## Cluster 2 — "diagnostic, not a cure" · `SEARCHED` · **repeated**

**Products:** Aimchess, Dr. Wolf, Chess.com Game Review.
**Representative:** *"excellent at identifying weaknesses and decent at providing drills, but the
actual improvement still comes from disciplined work"*; Dr. Wolf described by some users as *"not
much different than analyzing games with Chess.com"*.
**Counterexamples:** some users report Aimchess feedback *"feels personal instead of generic chess
advice"*, with sessions short enough not to be put off.
**Status:** the market has already named the failure mode this mission is about, on the product
closest to Decision Lab's current position. It does not tell us the fix.

## Cluster 3 — review backlog / spaced-repetition overload · `SEARCHED, shallow` · **repeated**

**Products:** Chessable, Anki.
The queue-growth complaint recurs wherever spacing is deployed. **This is the adherence cost of the
mechanism with the best learning evidence**, which is the §6 conflict in its clearest form: the
component most supported by V1/V11 is the component users most often abandon.
**Status:** friction signal. Bounded queues and stopping rules are the usual product answer, and
whether they corrupt the mechanism is untested.

## Clusters listed by the mission and `NOT SEARCHED` in this pass

- "I understand the mistake but repeat it"
- "I know the best move but not why"
- "too many statistics" / "not actionable" / "generic advice"
- "personalized but shallow"
- "memorizing solutions rather than learning"
- "training does not show up in games" *(adjacent to cluster 1; not separately searched)*
- "I like positions from my own games"
- "I like being told what to train next"
- "explanations are more valuable than scores"
- "training is boring / I would rather play"
- "hints create dependency" / "too much guidance" / "not enough guidance"
- "daily plan creates a training habit"

**These are absent, not empty.** Several bear directly on H7 (own-error training) and H15
(learning vs adherence), and a fuller pass would change how confidently those two are stated —
which is why both are recorded as `UNRESOLVED` rather than answered.

## What VOC changed in this research

**One thing, and it is significant.** Cluster 1 raised trigger focality from *"a mechanism in the
prospective-memory literature"* to *"a mechanism in the literature that users independently describe
as the thing that goes wrong"*. Convergence between a tier-1 source and a repeated tier-6 signal,
arrived at independently, is the strongest form this file can produce.

**What it did not change:** nothing here is causal evidence, and no intervention is justified by
this file alone.
