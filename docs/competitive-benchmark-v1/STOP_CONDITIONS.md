# STOP_CONDITIONS

## Before launch — hard no-go

- any deliverable missing or internally inconsistent;
- DL commit differs from freeze;
- selected competitor feature/plan cannot be reproduced from capability snapshot;
- item-bank hash absent, leakage suspected, or accepted moves not adjudicated;
- rubric alpha <0.67 after one repair pilot;
- randomization/allocation concealment not tested;
- instrumentation cannot distinguish product-caused from research-caused failure.

## During pilot

Stop and repair apparatus if:

- research-caused blocking failure >10%;
- median task duration exceeds cap by >20%;
- >15% items floor (<20% correct) or ceiling (>90% correct);
- moderator intervention occurs in >10% sessions;
- delayed-window adherence <80%.

Pilot repair creates an amendment and fresh pilot items. Pilot outcomes never support a competitive claim.

## Confirmatory

- administrative maximum: 120 completers; blinded variance re-estimation may raise to 160.
- pause on privacy/fair-play incident.
- pause if research infrastructure failure >10% in either arm.
- competitor/DL outage affecting >20% of scheduled sessions: extend windows once; if asymmetry persists, invalidate.
- product-native errors are not a stop by themselves; they are outcomes unless data integrity is lost.
- no early efficacy stop.
- optional futility stop at 90 completers only if blinded statistician finds conditional power <10% for reaching +8pp under the pre-specified model; decision documented before unblinding investigators.

## Claim stopping

A claim is killed when its maximum-sample 95% CI upper bound is below its MID, even if p<.05 for a smaller effect. A claim is supported only at its named threshold. Otherwise unresolved. No relabeling a failed primary outcome as success via secondary metrics.
