# TASK_PROTOCOL

## עקרונות

- כל זרוע מקבלת 3 יחידות של 25 דקות בתוך 7 ימים.
- אין עזרה בזמן החלטת מבחן; אין engine/network access.
- אותו hardware class ככל האפשר; viewport, language, time control ו-network logging נשמרים.
- selector של עמדות המבחן עצמאי מכל מוצר ומכל output של DL.
- לכל mechanism family יש trigger items ו-matched non-trigger controls.
- אין reuse של FEN, opening line או tactical sequence בין training ל-test.

## T0 — Intake and baseline

1. verify account/rating/activity.
2. collect prior-tool exposure, English proficiency, device.
3. 12 positions, 90 seconds each.
4. response order: move → reason (max 240 chars) → confidence 1–7.
5. no feedback. Autosave; timeout is a response, not exclusion.

## T1–T3 — Native workflow

המשתתף מקבל launcher עם משימה אחת: "השתמשו בכלי כדי להפיק מהמשחקים שלכם דבר אחד שישנה החלטה עתידית."  
אין שימוש במילים ownership, epistemic, falsification או transfer.

- DL uses frozen product, including required commitments and reveals.
- BAA uses full paid plan and frozen step-card specific to the selected product.
- Both arms receive same 25-minute cap.
- Setup/import time is measured separately and included in product burden.
- Moderator may solve access only; no chess interpretation, reframing or prompting.
- Product-caused inability to finish counts as non-completion under ITT.

## T4 — Immediate test

12 parallel unseen positions, same response sequence. Item families balanced; no item used at baseline.

## T5 — Delayed test

24 items after 72±12h:

- 12 trigger items across 4 families;
- 12 matched non-trigger items;
- near and far surface similarity balanced;
- 90 seconds/item;
- no feedback until all responses frozen.

Primary correctness accepts any move approved ex ante by the independent chess panel, not only engine top-1. Post hoc engine alternatives cannot widen acceptance after unblinding.

## T6 — Prospective play

10 new rated games or 14 days. No in-game intervention added by researchers. Eligible opportunities and recurrence labels are generated by the frozen external classifier and audited blind on 20%.

## T7 — Free choice

After T5 is locked:

1. 5-minute neutral familiarization with the product not yet used.
2. participant receives 7 days access to both.
3. no reminders naming a product.
4. measure first choice, active minutes, completed meaningful sessions and day-7 return.

## Item-bank construction

Three independent titled/2000+ reviewers propose families without seeing product outputs. Each item requires:

- FEN, side to move, source/license;
- accepted move set;
- family and trigger yes/no;
- difficulty estimate;
- surface-distance tier;
- one-sentence ground truth and boundary condition;
- engine/version/depth provenance;
- adjudicator agreement.

Items with reviewer disagreement after one adjudication are removed, not rewritten.
