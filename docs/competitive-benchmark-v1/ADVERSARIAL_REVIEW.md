## Order, selection and learning

Parallel assignment protects the confirmatory endpoint. Crossover occurs only after T5 is locked. Item order is randomized within balanced blocks; no family repeats in adjacent items; baseline/immediate/delayed forms are disjoint. Reminders and time windows are identical. Analysts see arms only after frozen derivations pass.

## Residual weaknesses

- Participants cannot be blinded to UI.
- Native workflows cannot have identical inputs without destroying ecological validity.
- 72h transfer is not long-term improvement.
- BAA bundle may be less coherent than a single product.
- Human coaching remains outside v1.
- Product changes after the freeze limit external validity.

These do not disappear in analysis; they bound the claim.
